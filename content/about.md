About dmx.berlin
================

dmx.berlin is the personal website of Jörg Richter, software developer. He lives and works, and was born, in Berlin. All his software is Open Source.

<image src="/Joerg-Richter.jpg" width="140px" float="left"></image>

After abandoning his LEGO bricks Jörg discovered the computer as the ultimate tool for endless puzzles. That was 1981 when he learned Z80 assembler on a MPF-I. A year later he did Pac Man and Centipede clones on his ZX Spectrum. When at high school he created MIDI sound editors for Roland keyboards and sold them through G.C.Geerdes and Softarts. After studying computer science and linguistics, in the late 90s he worked at Claassen & Friends and built one of the largest websites about contemporary arts, [artfacts.net](https://artfacts.net). At 2000 he was employed at Staps Mindware when *Knowledge Management* was the thing. Matthias Staps set him on a mission: "Do research about Knowledge Management and this is how the UI should look like", he showed me a Mind Map. This was the birth of the *DeepaMehta* project. After the company collapsed, Mathias allowed me to continue the project as Open Source. Since then the quest of building a user interface for knowledge workers never left him. After his own companies (*DeepaMehta GmbH* and *DMX Systems UG*) collapsed as well, his passion is unbroken, and since 2025 a complete rewrite, [version 6](/project/dm6-elm), is on the way.

Besides software development Jörg's passion is music. In the 80s he grew up with the punk and new wave records of his sister. In the late 80s he started experimenting with a Roland D-10 synthesizer, bought from a small inheritance from his grandmother. In the early 90s he did a duo with a former high school buddy. Both efforts are now documented at Bandcamp as [Kind aus Blech](https://kind-aus-blech.bandcamp.com) and [Phil & Jörg](https://phil-and-joerg.bandcamp.com) respectively. Since the 2000's he was regular guest DJ at [MissVergnügen](https://www.eschschloraque.de/missvergnuegen)'s *Strange Music for Strange People* parties at [Eschschloraque](https://www.eschschloraque.de). Today he's curating 2 music streams, one featuring experimental 80s DIY music, [Essential Madness Radio](https://emr.fm), the other featuring meditative music, [Bhakta Radio](https://emr.fm/bhakta).

Jörg Richter is available for hiring.  
Most he loves developing in the programming language [Elm](https://elm-lang.org).
